Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This notice explains how Pitchball handles information when you use pitchball.gr and its related pages and features.

Who is responsible

The website operator and data controller is Thanos Chatziiordanou, based in Greece. For privacy questions or requests, email thanos19chatz@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account information: username, email address, and password verifier used for sign-in.
  • Things you submit: comments, replies, reactions, predictions, profile-image links, and other information you choose to provide. Comments and usernames may be visible to other visitors.
  • Browser storage: the site uses local storage for account session details, cookie choices, and some feature preferences or saved data.
  • Technical information: our hosting and service providers may receive connection data such as IP address, browser/device information, and request logs.

Why we use it

We use information to operate accounts and site features, display user-submitted content, protect and maintain the service, and respond to requests. Optional Google Analytics is used only if you accept analytics in the cookie notice. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Services and sharing

Site features use Google Analytics (when accepted), Google Fonts, Firebase Realtime Database, and JSONBin. These providers process information needed for their services under their own terms and privacy notices. Profile images are loaded from the image URL a user supplies, so that image host may receive a request from your browser. The site also links to third-party services; their privacy practices are separate.

Service providers may process information in other countries, including outside the European Economic Area. Their applicable transfer safeguards and terms should be reviewed before continued use of those services.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your analytics choice in your browser. Account sessions and some preferences are also stored in browser local storage. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure site use. Rejecting analytics prevents the site from loading Google Analytics. Analytics cookies already set may remain until they expire or you remove them in your browser settings.

Retention and your choices

Account information and associated content are kept while the account remains in use, or until a request is made to remove it, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate or legal reasons. There is not currently an automated account-deletion control. To ask for access, correction, or deletion, email us from the address associated with the account. Requests are handled manually and may require reasonable verification.

You may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, request data portability, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority at dpa.gr.

Children

Public pages are available to all ages, but accounts are intended only for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly seek to create accounts for people under 16. If you believe a person under 16 has created an account, contact us at the email above.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the site or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page. For questions, email thanos19chatz@gmail.com.
